# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in a Bates Solutions repository: in issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be unprofessional in a workplace.

## Reporting

Email **contact@bates-solutions.com**. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block
someone from the project, temporarily or permanently.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
